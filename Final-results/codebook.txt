CODEBOOK: WHO adolescent physical activity estimates, 2010-2025

Estimates of physical activity among adolescents aged 11-17 years, by year,
sex and area, for 195 countries and territories and for global, regional and
income-group aggregates.

Source: Strain, T, Flaxman SR, Ricardo, LI, Guthold R, et al. National, regional, and global trends in adolescent physical
activity from 2010 to 2025: a pooled analysis of 389 surveys with 3.2 million participants from 140 countries. Lancet
Global Health, 2026. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.langlo.2026.104099

An "active day" is a day with at least 60 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous
physical activity (MVPA).

WARNING: Country estimates where publish = "no" should NOT be presented or
reported. These countries have no included survey data. Their estimates are
provided so that users can calculate estimates for custom groupings of
countries (see CALCULATING CUSTOM REGIONS below).
*******************************************************************************

All files cover the years 2010-2025.

Rows are not additive. Each file contains estimates for boys, girls and both
sexes combined, and the regions files contain several overlapping types of
region. Select one value of sex (and one value of regiontype) before
comparing or combining rows.

Aggregates include all 195 countries and territories, including those with
publish = "no". Some countries do not belong to a group in every grouping:

- Taiwan, China (TWN) is not a WHO Member State (who_region = "Not a WHO
  Member State"). It is included in the global, income-group and analysis-
  region aggregates but not in any WHO region.
- Cook Islands (COK) and Niue (NIU) have no 2025 World Bank income
  classification (income_group = "Not classified"). They are included in the
  global, WHO region and analysis-region aggregates but not in any income
  group.


CALCULATING CUSTOM REGIONS

Estimates for a custom group of countries can be calculated as the
population-weighted average of the country estimates:

    estimate for group = sum(estimate x population) / sum(population)

using the country rows for the same year, sex (and age) in the countries file.
Include all countries in the group, whether publish is "yes" or "no".

This gives the point estimate (mean_days or pct_Xdays) only. Uncertainty
intervals for a custom group cannot be calculated from the _lwr and _upr
columns; they require the posterior draws.
